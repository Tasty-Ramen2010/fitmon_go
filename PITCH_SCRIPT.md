# FitMon GO: 2:30 pitch script (TSA CTSO Day, Georgia)

The **[Show: …]** lines tell you which picture from `design/screenshots/` to put on screen. When a section lists several pictures, change to the next one on each new sentence. The times assume a calm pace of about 145 words a minute.

---

## 0:00–0:20 · The hook

**[Show: nothing yet. A black slide, or just you.]**

> Four in ten American adults have obesity. For kids, it's about one in five.
>
> It's not that we don't know exercise matters. We just don't *want* to do it. The World Health Organization says 1.8 billion adults don't move enough, and that number keeps going up.

## 0:20–0:40 · The problem

> Meanwhile, teens spend more than eight hours a day on screens for fun, and 85% of them play video games.
>
> Games are great at one thing: making you come back tomorrow. Workouts aren't. They're repetitive, your progress is invisible, and quitting is easy.

## 0:40–0:55 · The idea

**[Show: `00a-pitch-hero.png`]**

> So we asked: what if the workout *was* the game? This is FitMon GO. Catch reps, not monsters.
>
> It's built on three ideas: make exercise fun, make progress visible, and make it social.

## 0:55–1:50 · How it works

**[Show: `00b-how-it-plays.png`]**

> It plays like Pokémon GO. You walk around, and instead of creatures, *exercises* spawn near you.

**[Show: `01-explore-map.png`]**

> Push-ups and squats are common. Pull-ups are rare. A front lever is mythic.

**[Show: `02-mythic-encounter.png`]**

> To catch one, you do the reps. Your camera checks your form, so every catch is earned.

**[Show: `03-street-mode.png`, then `04-gym-mode.png`]**

> On the street, you get walking and running missions, never in the road. At a gym, you pick a muscle and take on a raid.

**[Show: `05-fitdex.png`, then `06-trainer-stats.png`]**

> Every catch goes into your FitDex and levels up real stats: strength, speed and endurance.

**[Show: `12-battle-deck.png`, then `07-online-arena.png`, then `08-finisher-unlocked.png`]**

> Those stats become your moves. Build a deck and battle players online. Master a hard skill like the muscle-up, and you unlock a finisher.

**[Show: `09-duel-invite.png`, then `10-duel-live.png`, then `11-duel-result.png`]**

> Meet a player in real life? Street duel: best of three quick sets. The winner steals one of the loser's FitMon.

**[Show: `00c-street-duels.png`]**

> Rare catches are much harder to steal, so hard work is protected.

## 1:50–2:15 · Why it works and who it helps

**[Keep showing: `00c-street-duels.png`, or go back to `01-explore-map.png`]**

> We know this works. A Stanford and Microsoft study found Pokémon GO players walked about 1,500 more steps a day. FitMon GO goes further, with full workouts.
>
> And the need is huge. Three out of four kids don't get the hour of activity they need each day. That's tens of millions of U.S. kids, and almost every teen already has the phone to play.

## 2:15–2:30 · The close

**[Show: `00a-pitch-hero.png`]**

> FitMon GO turns screen time into moving time: a collection you're proud of, stats you can see, and a game you want to win.
>
> Catch reps, not monsters. Thank you.

## Delivery tips

- Say the hook slowly, and pause for a beat after "one in five."
- The "How it works" section has the most pictures. Change the picture as each new sentence starts, instead of reading from it.
- If you run long, cut the gym-mode sentence first, then the finisher sentence.
