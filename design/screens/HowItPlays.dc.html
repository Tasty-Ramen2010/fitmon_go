How FitMon GO plays

Real movement in, game progress out.
STEP 1ExploreWalk your city. Exercise spawns and missions appear on the map.
STEP 2CatchDo the reps. The camera counts form, so every catch is earned.
STEP 3Level upEach catch trains a muscle group and boosts STR, END, SPD and more.
STEP 4BattleTake your stats, power-ups and finishers into live online duels.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
Street ModeON ROADS & IN PUBLICOnly safe run and walk missions spawn here. Distance, pace and hills level up Speed and Endurance.
Gym ModeAT A GYM OR IN A SESSIONPick a target muscle and run a raid of missions. Clearing sets earns points toward that muscle's level.
ArenaONLINE, ANYWHEREDuel other trainers with moves built from your stats. Use power-ups, charge your meter, land a finisher.
