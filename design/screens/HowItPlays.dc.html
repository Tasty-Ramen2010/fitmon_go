How FitMon GO plays

real sweat in, game progress out
step 1ExploreWalk your city. Exercise spawns and missions pop up on the map.
step 2CatchDo the reps. The camera checks your form, so every catch is earned.
step 3Level upEach catch trains a muscle and boosts Strength, Speed and more.
step 4BattleTake your stats, power-ups and finishers into duels.

Street modeOn roads & in publicOnly safe run and walk missions. Distance, pace and hills level up Speed and Endurance.
Gym modeAt a gym or in a sessionPick a target muscle and clear a raid of missions to level it up.
ArenaOnline, anywhereDuel trainers with moves built from your stats, power-ups and finishers.
